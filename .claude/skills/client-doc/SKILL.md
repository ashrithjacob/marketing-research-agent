# Writing for the client

These documents came back for a second and third pass more often than anything
else in this repo, and always for the same reasons. The corrections are below
so they do not have to be given again.

## Shape

A table of line items. One row per deliverable: **what is delivered**, and
**how much work**. Nothing else unless it is asked for.

| Work | Hours |
|---|---|
| Amazon star-filtered reviews — experiment to get past the block | 3 |

- **Markdown**, always, unless another format is named.
- **Hours, not days.** Half a day is one hour if that is what it is.
- One total at the bottom. Not a breakdown of totals by category — one number.
- No "free vs paid" split, no cost-model column, unless it was asked for.

## Voice

- **"I", not "we".** These are written as the person talking to their client.
- **"A decision I need"**, not "a decision we need to make".
- Plain words. Every abbreviation spelled out the first time it appears.
- Short. If a row can be a phrase it should not be a sentence, and no section
  should meander towards its point.

## Substance

- A limitation is stated, with what it would take to get past it. "Amazon
  blocks anything past the top ten reviews; getting past it is an experiment
  that may fail" is useful. "Amazon has limitations" is not.
- Never quietly leave a line the client asked to have removed. When something
  is removed or rescoped, it goes, everywhere, including any total it fed.
- Numbers in these documents get quoted back. Do not put one in without having
  measured it or being able to say where it came from.
