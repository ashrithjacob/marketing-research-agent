something i came across:

Even if you are doing long form statics rip-offs from competitor ads please do it the right way!

The way some brands are ripping ads is just fucking disgusting!

This is how pros are doing it:

1- Extract the Competitor "Chassis"

Don't write from scratch. Find a top-performing competitor ad on TrendTrack or Ad Library (even if their mechanism is slightly different).

Dump it into a trained Claude project that already holds your brand's research, ICP, and mechanism.

Ask Claude to analyze the competitor ad and extract the underlying psychological skeleton—the "chassis."

2. Spin the POV

Once you have the chassis, force the AI to write 3 different script variations using completely different Points of View (POV):

• 1st Person: The actual patient suffering from the issue.

• The Authority: A doctor or clinic worker.

• The Caregiver: A wife talking about her stubborn husband.

3. Inject "Human Intent" (The Secret Sauce)
This is where lazy media buyers fail.

AI hooks are naturally weak and generic. You MUST manually rewrite the hook to make it punchy and intense.

Read through the body to ensure the mechanism is 100% accurate to your product, and manually fix the PS/CTA section so the guarantee (e.g., 30-day vs 90-day) matches your exact offer.

4. Source "Ugly" Native Creatives

Slick, high-def graphics kill native CTR. Go to Pinterest. Find low-quality, raw photos (like a family dinner taken from behind so you don't see faces).

It needs to look exactly like a grainy, unedited photo a 60-year-old would post on their Facebook feed. Hiding faces also keeps you DMCA-proof.

6. The "Reddit / FB groups Fear" Scaler (Advanced Angle SOP)

Once your AI model is perfectly trained on your winning chassis, stop looking at competitors and go to Reddit to find hidden, deep-rooted fears.

• Example: We found out seniors were terrified of having a heart attack on a Caribbean cruise and getting hit with a $300k helicopter evacuation bill.

We fed that exact Reddit fear + our blood pressure mechanism into Claude. It spit out a massive, blue-ocean winning angle that competitors couldn't touch.
